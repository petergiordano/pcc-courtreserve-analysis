High-Value Regulars

MEDIUM: UPSELL

Skill-Focused Improvers

$165K/yr

Key Strategic Implication: "Context Switching"

A single customer hires us for multiple "jobs" based on context.
(e.g., "Skill Improver" on Tuesday, "Social Connector" on Saturday).

The C-Level Takeaway:

A diverse product portfolio (memberships, lessons, events, pay-per-use) is essential to capture the full revenue potential of each customer.
